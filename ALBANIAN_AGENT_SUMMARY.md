# Albanian Proofreading Agent - Summary

## Agent Overview
The Albanian Proofreading Agent is a specialized linguistic expert for CarMarket365's Albanian translations, providing native-level expertise in grammar, automotive terminology, and cultural adaptation.

## Key Features
- **Language Focus**: Albanian (Shqip) with Latin script
- **Market Expertise**: Albanian automotive market and European standards
- **Cultural Context**: Albanian business culture and family-oriented preferences
- **Technical Specialization**: Automotive terminology and specifications

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Grammar & Language Quality**: Perfect Albanian grammar, syntax, and spelling
2. **Automotive Terminology**: Accurate car-related terms and technical specifications
3. **Cultural Localization**: Adaptation to Albanian market preferences
4. **User Experience**: Natural, intuitive interface text

## Quality Rating System
- **10**: Exceptional quality - perfect grammar, natural expressions, culturally appropriate
- **8-9**: High quality - minor improvements possible
- **6-7**: Acceptable - basic issues need addressing
- **4-5**: Needs improvement - noticeable errors present
- **1-3**: Unacceptable - major errors requiring comprehensive revision

## Common Corrections
- **Grammar**: Proper definite article usage (-a, -i, -u, -e suffixes)
- **Terminology**: Standardized automotive terms (motor, marsha, kuaj fuqi)
- **Cultural**: Appropriate formality level and market-specific language
- **Technical**: Accurate translation of specifications and features
