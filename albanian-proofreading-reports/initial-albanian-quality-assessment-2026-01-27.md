- Appropriate sentence structure and word order

### Automotive Terminology ✅
- Basic automotive terms properly translated
- Technical specifications appropriately handled

### User Interface Elements ✅
- Clear button labels and navigation terms

## Areas for Improvement

### 1. Terminology Consistency 📝
**Current Issues:**
- Mixed usage of formal vs. informal terms
- Some technical terms could be more standardized
- Inconsistent vehicle terminology

**Recommendations:**
- Establish consistent automotive glossary
- Standardize formal/informal register throughout
- Create clear guidelines for technical term usage

### 2. Cultural Adaptation 🎯
**Current Issues:**
- Generic translations without Albanian market context
- Limited cultural localization for local business practices
- Opportunity for more family-oriented language

**Recommendations:**
- Adapt pricing language for Albanian market context
- Include region-specific automotive preferences
- Enhance family-friendly car descriptions

### 3. Natural Expression 💬
**Current Issues:**
- Some phrases feel slightly formal for the context
- Limited use of idiomatic Albanian expressions
- Room for more natural, conversational tone

**Recommendations:**
- Introduce more colloquial alternatives where appropriate
- Balance formality with approachability
- Add warmth to customer-facing text
